About SMS-USCE

Clinical experience beyond borders

SMS-USCE is an international medical education consultancy specializing in facilitating U.S. clinical rotations for medical students and graduates. This opportunity is particularly beneficial to students who intend to embark on the USMLE journey and subsequently apply for residency training in the United States after medical school, as U.S. clinical experience is a valuable component of residency applications.

However, it is not limited to such students. It is equally open to those who simply wish to experience the American healthcare system and broaden their medical knowledge without necessarily pursuing the USMLE or residency training in the United States.

SMS-USCE is a team of Ghanaian, Nigerian and American doctors, together with other administrative team members, who facilitate the entire process, from passport acquisition and visa application guidance to securing clinical rotation placements across various medical and surgical specialties in the United States.
